Basic Mechanics

Knowledge
Knowledge is used to unlock other upgrades, a core important resource generated by clicking
Mood
Mood affects how fast your bars move, along with if it's very bad, stops you from generating more knowledge (It's recommended to get the mood bar). So if you can't generate knowledge/wisdom check your mood.
Inventions
Technically you can call them upgrades, but anyway their requirements to unlock are hidden! Some take knowledge/wisdom (BUT NEVER BONUSES!) or levels of bars. Since the 0.50.0 update, there can be multi requirements or costs

If you're ever stuck and no upgrades are visible, you can click the find a random unlock (if you have enough) and it'll say a random unlock requirements in the story page.


Specific tip: This game unfolds as you go. There is a lot of content that unlocks. Sometimes you may feel lost, and that's ok.

The point of this game is to carve your own personal path. You may miss an upgrade or something, but that's the exact point. Discover something hella cool your personal own way.


Advanced Mechanics

The Pit
Throw your knowledge and wisdom into it to potientally recieve stuff back or nothing. Wisdom is better to throw FYI
Bonuses (+0)
Bonuses are the numbers next to your resources. Gotten only from the pit, it's not taxed by mood and only used when you cannot normally afford an upgrade. Is not thrown into the pit aswell
Magic
Many people say to me that they don't know what it does, but you must find out! (Hint: level it up)
Matter/Creation
The creation bar gives you +1 matter every time it levels up. While active, it makes your mood capacity be able to take 1/2 as much as normal.
